## Description

CGMP v1.4 defines a published digital taxi meter using vehicle-class distance rates, Rs 12.90/minute verified passenger-trip time, a 2 km passenger minimum component, a 2 km pickup minimum component, 7% platform commission, deterministic passenger-authorized search expansion, no automatic scarcity multiplier and no discretionary driver bidding.

Working class rates are Bike Rs 22/km, Tuk Rs 37/km, Mini Rs 52/km, Compact Rs 57/km and Sedan Rs 65/km. Individual vehicle efficiency gains remain with the driver.

Passenger cancellation after pickup travel can settle validated pickup compensation to the driver under a published grace/no-show rule. Driver personal stops require time billing to be paused with notification to both parties.

Long-distance displacement treatment begins after 40 passenger kilometres. It accrues progressively using published marginal bands, remains provisional during the trip, and is reconciled at completion. The ordinary meter is not clawed back. Exact marginal rates remain a calibration item before a live charging pilot.

Cash trips use a commission ledger with a published settlement threshold. Return-direction outcomes are measured at 6, 12 and 24 hours after long-distance dropoff.
