## Abstract / description

Cost-Grounded Mobility Pricing (CGMP) is an open ride-hailing pricing and dispatch framework that separates ordinary trip pricing from exceptional scarcity resolution. The ordinary fare is calculated from published vehicle-distance, passenger-trip time, pickup, and rule-derived long-distance displacement components. Scarcity does not automatically change this baseline through an opaque multiplier. If the baseline fails to attract a driver, the passenger may provide a private maximum fare while driver applications submit private offers generated from preconfigured economic preferences. The first server-valid offer within the passenger ceiling clears immediately. The framework also allows standing passenger authorization for pickup distance and cost, reducing checkout friction while preserving informed consent. CGMP is therefore a hybrid of cost-grounded posted pricing and speed-oriented sealed market clearing. The framework is presented as a testable design proposal rather than a proven replacement for dynamic surge pricing; simulation and controlled field trials are proposed to evaluate passenger, driver, matching, safety, and platform outcomes.
