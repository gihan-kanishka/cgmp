# Long-Distance and Displacement Policy

## Principle

CGMP guarantees a fair outbound fare. It does not guarantee a profitable round trip.

Ordinary distance and time charges compensate the actual outbound trip. An additional long-distance provision is permitted only for a defined, necessary displacement cost caused by the trip.

## Potentially eligible costs

Depending on the market and route:

- accommodation where immediate safe return is unreasonable;
- a defined meal allowance associated with such a stay;
- unavoidable tolls or route-specific fees;
- another published displacement cost category.

## Not automatically compensated

CGMP does not automatically add full empty-return vehicle cost, hypothetical lost future wages, guaranteed round-trip profit, or discretionary after-the-fact driver claims.

## Pre-trip calculation and consent

Any displacement provision must be determined before trip acceptance from published rules, based on known or estimable route/departure/ETA/vehicle factors, shown to the passenger, and accepted before dispatch.

## Safe-return decision

A deployment may compare a reasonable same-day return provision with a stay provision, but safety and feasibility override a purely monetary comparison.

## Route overrides

Markets may define documented route-severity overrides for destinations where terrain, weather, road conditions or vehicle restrictions make same-day return assumptions inappropriate.

If the baseline plus rule-derived provision still attracts no driver, the trip may enter the sealed-bid process.
